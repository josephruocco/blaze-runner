# Opening Sequence and First Night

## Goal

Teach the entire premise through play in roughly five minutes: ordinary taxi work by day, souls after dark, monsters in pursuit, and the haunted car offering dangerous help.

## Cold open

Black screen. Rain and an engine that will not turn over.

> LAST FARE OF THE DAY.
>
> THEN THE CAR IS YOURS.

The engine starts by itself. Two cyan headlights appear in the dark. The title resolves:

> GHOST TAXI
>
> GRAVEYARD SHIFT

The player begins parked outside the taxi garage at 5:40 PM.

## Day tutorial: one last fare

### Pickup

A living passenger waves from the curb. A single prompt appears: **PICK UP FARE**.

The passenger is going to Riverside Station before 6:00 PM. The route teaches steering, acceleration, braking, and the destination marker. There are no enemies.

Passenger dialogue stays short and appears above the objective band:

> This cab always smell this cold?

The player earns a fare and tip on arrival. The passenger exits, then looks back at the empty rear seat.

> You know somebody's still in there, right?

### Sunset

At 6:00 PM, the town transitions in place. Shops close, traffic thins, streetlights flicker, and fog rolls from the cemetery. The radio dissolves into static.

The taxi locks its own doors. The HAUNT meter appears for the first time.

> NIGHT SHIFT STARTED

## Night tutorial: first soul

A cyan figure waits at the abandoned hospital taxi stand. The figure is readable as a passenger, not an enemy. Picking it up changes the destination to Riverside Cemetery and slowly raises HAUNT.

The passenger is **Mara, the Racer**. Her spirit meter fades during the trip.

> I missed the last ride home.
>
> Let me help with the corners.

The player's first sharp turn triggers a mild steering assist and cyan tire trail. A compact card appears:

> RACER SOUL
>
> PERFECT DRIFT

## First pursuit

Halfway to the cemetery, two ghouls emerge from alleys and a monster car wakes behind the player. They pursue and ram but do not shoot.

The PHASE button unlocks. Its first use is free and passes the taxi through one obstacle while sharply raising HAUNT.

At high HAUNT, the taxi accelerates harder, cyan eyes appear in its headlights, and the wheel occasionally pulls toward a side street. This introduces the central idea: possession is powerful and dangerous.

## Delivery and binding

At the cemetery gate, Mara leaves the rear seat but remains reflected in the taxi's mirror.

> I can take the wheel when you need me.

The player receives the first permanent soul:

> MARA BOUND
>
> ACTIVE: AUTO-DRIFT

Dawn breaks. Monsters dissolve into fog and normal traffic begins returning.

## First hex

Back at the garage, the mechanic finds the steering wheel turning on its own. The player receives **Phantom Steering**.

- Drawback: the car occasionally pulls toward haunted roads.
- Benefit: completing a drift charges a one-hit spectral shield.

For the first level this hex is automatically bound, making the relationship between curse and power explicit. Later levels offer cleanse-or-bind choices.

## End of tutorial

The next living passenger gets into the taxi during daylight. Mara is faintly visible beside them.

> DAY 2
>
> THE DEAD RIDE FREE. THE LIVING STILL TIP.

Control returns immediately. The full day/night loop begins.

## Tutorial constraints

- Never stop play for more than one short line of text.
- Introduce only one control or system at a time.
- Guarantee the first soul pickup and cemetery route.
- Delay monster spawning until the player has demonstrated basic steering.
- Prevent failure during the day tutorial.
- The first capture returns the taxi to the road with damage instead of ending the run.
- Keep the entire sequence skippable after it has been completed once.
